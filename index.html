Arch Linux Backup

Arch Linux Backup is a personal backup tool. It uses rclone to copy files from my own Arch Linux computer to my own Google Drive.

It is not a public service. It is used only by its owner, and the backups are stored privately in the owner's Google Drive account.

What it does

  • Signs in to Google Drive with Google's standard sign-in (OAuth).
  • Uploads backup copies of files from the owner's computer to the owner's Drive.
  • Can download those backups again to restore files.
